Sources of the charge transfer reaction fits in chargetransfer.txt.

The script makechargetransferfile in the artisatomic repository downloads
the sources named below and generates the file. Run it to regenerate the
file from the current sources.

The fit form for every entry is equation (7) of Kingdon & Ferland (1996):
  k = a * 1e-9 * t4^b * (1 + c * exp(d * t4)) * exp(-eexp/T)  [cm3/s]
with t4 = T/1e4 K clamped into [tmin, tmax]. The Boltzmann factor
exp(-eexp/T) uses the true temperature and applies to some entries of the
ionisation direction.

1. Reactions with atomic hydrogen (recombination X^q+ + H0, and
   ionisation X^q+ + H+):
   Kingdon & Ferland (1996), ApJS, 106, 205, Tables 1-3, with the later
   updates that the Cloudy code applies to individual reactions. The
   entries come from the Cloudy master data files ctrecombdata.dat and
   ctiondata.dat (gitlab.nublado.org, retrieved 2026-08-20). The comment
   of each updated line names the update and keeps the original KF96
   values. An entry with a = 1e-05 is the 1e-14 cm3/s radiative floor of
   an endothermic reaction, and an entry with a = 0 marks an endothermic
   reaction with no fit (KF96 Table 4).
   Cloudy replaces a few of these fits at run time with special-case
   polynomials outside the fitted range (e.g. the O0 + H+ rates of
   Stancil et al. 1999). This file keeps only the clamped fits.

2. Reactions with neutral helium (recombination X^q+ + He0):
   Arnaud & Rothenflug (1985), A&AS, 60, 425, from the ASCII table ct2.dat
   of D. Verner (pa.uky.edu/~verner/chtr.html, retrieved 2026-08-20). The
   comments name the reactions where Cloudy replaces an AR85 fit with a
   newer constant rate.

3. Reactions of the n-capture elements Ge, Se, Br, Kr, Rb, and Xe with
   hydrogen:
   Sterling & Stancil (2011), A&A, 535, A117, from the machine-readable
   tables of CDS catalogue J/A+A/535/A117. That paper publishes tabulated
   k(T) values and no fit coefficients, so the entries here are least
   squares fits of the form a * t4^b * exp(-eexp/T) over 1e3 to 4e4 K.
   The comment of each entry gives the maximum fit error. Above tmax the
   clamp underestimates some of these rates, because they keep rising
   steeply where charge transfer is subdominant.

The code generates estimates at startup for the reactions that this file
does not cover (see chargetransfer.cc). An exothermic capture from a
neutral donor by an ion with a charge of one to three, with an energy
defect up to 4 eV, gets the flat near-resonant rate of 1e-9 cm3/s
(Melius 1974, J. Phys. B, 7, 1692). The other captures by an ion with a
charge of two or more get a Landau-Zener estimate. That method is the
one of Butler & Dalgarno (1980), ApJ, 241, 838, which Kingdon & Ferland
(1996) also used for their reactions without quantal data.

Known gap: KF96 give no rate for the exothermic ionisation of Ca+, Sc+,
and Ti+ by protons (the recombination direction is endothermic, KF96
Table 4). These reactions are absent here as well.
